Instructions, scoring and limitations

1. Search for a song: exact title matches auto-select. The app attempts to find lyrics, pitch charts and playable YouTube videos. Or paste a YouTube link: the app reads its metadata, tries to identify lyrics and finds charts. You can always load local media or note charts.

2. Enable the microphone and start playback. The glowing line is your detected pitch; purple bars are target notes. A score is available only with an UltraStar chart. Freestyle/rap notes are displayed but do not receive pitch scores.

3. The score is based on timed voiced frames: each note awards up to 100% accuracy when the sung note is near its target and held through its duration. Golden notes receive double weight. Missing notes count as zero while playback is active. Seeking backwards or changing timing settings resets your performance score.

4. YouTube music videos, live versions, and karaoke covers often have different timing from the chart. Use note and lyric shifts to align. UltraStar timing uses quarter-beat subdivisions (60/(BPM×4)); first voice only is supported. Some legacy relative layouts and mid-song BPM changes may not be supported.

Mic access requires a secure context (HTTPS or localhost). Audio files and mic input stay local. YouTube and online chart lookup need internet and may be restricted by network or embed availability. Your microphone input stays local. Scoring requires actual target notes; lyrics alone are not enough.
